I am someone who is curious about a broad array of subjects, and I
write about them here.

I recently graduated with a Ph.D. in Applied Physics with a focus on
Synthetic Biology, and am currently tinkering on my next project. 

During my Ph.D., I worked in Michael Elowitz's lab at Caltech
designing and building biological circuits. I have co-written academic
papers on a toolkit of [miRNA regulatory
circuits](https://doi.org/10.1101/2024.03.12.583048), applying miRNA
regulatory circuits to [Rett Syndrome gene
therapy](https://doi.org/10.1101/2024.03.13.584179), protein circuits
for [cancer therapy](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.04.16.647665), an
RT-LAMP-based [COVID
diagnostic](https://doi.org/10.1101/2020.11.19.20234948), and X-rays
generated by [magnetic
reconnection](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.5054927) in a laboratory
generated plasma jet.

I am a named inventor on patents for [miRNA regulatory circuits in
gene therapies](https://patents.google.com/patent/US12297236B2/) and
[biological
amplifiers](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20240124913A1/), and
I'm quite proud of them.
