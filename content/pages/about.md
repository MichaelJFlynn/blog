I am someone who is very curious about a broad array of subjects. I
write about them here. I recently graduated with a Ph.D. in Applied
Physics with a focus on Synthetic Biology. I am currently tinkering on
my next project.

I have co-written academic papers about [a toolkit of miRNA regulatory
circuits](https://doi.org/10.1101/2024.03.12.583048), [applying miRNA
regulatory circuits to Rett Syndrome gene
therapy](https://doi.org/10.1101/2024.03.13.584179), a [RT-LAMP based
field COVID diagnostic](https://doi.org/10.1101/2020.11.19.20234948),
and [X-rays generated by magnetic reconnection in a laboratory
generated plasma jet](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.5054927).

I am an author on patents for [miRNA regulatory circuits in gene
therapies](https://patents.google.com/patent/US12297236B2/) and
[biological
amplifiers](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20240124913A1/), and
quite proud of them.

I love books.
